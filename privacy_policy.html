Policy Details
1 Overview & Ownership
This Privacy Policy governs the ONE Authenticator application, published by ONE Studio and created by developer Rafat Ahmed. We respect your privacy and process all data in compliance with strict cybersecurity standards.
2 Data Storage & Local First
Your 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) accounts operate on a local-first principle:
  • Secret keys are stored in encrypted local storage (`SharedPreferences`).
  • TOTP 6-digit codes are calculated offline on your processor.
  • Clearing app data removes local tokens from the device.
3 Camera & Photo Permissions
We access device hardware solely to provide core functionality:
  • Camera: Used exclusively to scan 2FA setup QR codes.
  • Gallery/Album: Accesses selected images only when choosing a custom profile picture or scanning a saved QR code image.
4 Firebase Cloud Backup
Cloud backup uses Firebase Realtime Database to protect against data loss:
  • Data is organized strictly under your authenticated User ID (`users/{UID}`).
  • Only your signed-in account can read or write to your personal database node.
5 Biometric Security

When enabled, App Lock uses Android's native Biometric API (`androidx.biometric`). Biometric credentials (fingerprint or face) are processed inside your device's hardware security module and are never collected or transmitted.

6 Data Deletion & Control
You maintain complete authority over your stored records:
  • Deleting a 2FA account removes it permanently from both local storage and cloud database.
  • Deleting a custom avatar reverts the profile to your default Google account photo.
