Comprehensive Privacy Disclosures
1 Introduction & Ownership

Welcome to ONE Authenticator ("we", "our", or "us"), published by ONE Studio and architected by lead developer Rafat Ahmed. ONE Authenticator is a dedicated 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) security utility designed to generate Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP) compliant with RFC 6238 standards.

Your digital security is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy provides full transparency on how data is handled, stored, encrypted, and synchronized across your devices.

2 Information We Process

To provide 2FA token generation and account recovery, ONE Authenticator processes the following minimal information:

  • Authentication Account Credentials: Your account email, display name, and profile picture (when signed into your user session).
  • 2FA Account Records: Service Name (e.g., Facebook, Google), Account Identifier (e.g., email or username), Base32 Secret Key, and chosen Avatar Accent Color.
  • Security Preferences: App lock states, screen security flags, and theme customization choices.
3 How Your Data is Stored Safely

We implement a dual-layer, local-first hybrid storage system designed to maximize both offline speed and cloud safety:

🔐 Encrypted Local Storage: All account secrets are stored locally in private application memory (`SharedPreferences`). No other installed applications on your phone can access or read these records.
☁️ Secure Cloud Database Isolation: When signed in, your accounts are synchronized with our secure cloud database strictly under your unique account identifier (`users/{UID}`).

Our database security rules strictly enforce Account-Based Access Control. This ensures that even if another user uses the app on a different device, they can never read, modify, or intercept your cloud records.

4 Zero-Knowledge Offline TOTP Generation

Your 6-digit verification codes are calculated locally on your smartphone's processor using HMAC-SHA1 algorithms. An active internet connection is never required to generate 2FA codes. Your raw secret keys are never transmitted to unauthenticated third-party servers.

5 Hardware Permissions & Usage

To deliver core authentication features, ONE Authenticator requests access to specific device hardware:

  • Camera Permission: Used strictly for scanning 2FA QR setup codes. Video feeds are processed in real-time RAM and are never recorded or saved.
  • Photo Album & Media: Used only when you manually select a saved QR image or upload a custom profile picture. We do not access or read unselected photos.
  • Biometric Hardware: Used for App Lock. Biometric validation (fingerprint or face) is processed exclusively inside Android's Hardware Security Module (TEE) and is never transmitted to any external server.
6 Screen Security & Privacy Controls

When you enable "Block Screenshots" in settings, ONE Authenticator applies system-level security protection:

  • Prevents unauthorized screenshots or screen recordings of your 2FA codes.
  • Automatically blanks out the app preview in Android's Recent Apps / Multitasking switcher.
7 Data Control & Permanent Deletion

You maintain 100% control over your stored accounts and data lifecycle:

  • Permanent Account Deletion: Deleting a 2FA record in the app removes it instantly from local device storage and overwrites the record in the cloud database.
  • Profile Picture Removal: Deleting a custom avatar removes the record from cloud storage and restores your default profile photo.
  • App Data Wiping: Clearing application data or signing out purges local tokens. Signing back in restores your encrypted cloud backup.
8 Third-Party Infrastructure

We rely on secure cloud authentication and database infrastructure. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or commercial data brokers.

9 Policy Changes & Compliance

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect security enhancements or Google Play Store policy requirements. Continued usage of ONE Authenticator indicates acceptance of the updated policy terms.
